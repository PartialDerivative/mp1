About Me

I'm a game designer and self-taught developer who loves building small, sharp tactics and puzzle games. I care about tight combat systems, readable UI, and mechanics that reward planning over luck — then I build the whole thing myself, front to back.

This page is my MP1 submission for CS409 — every section demonstrates a required technique: sticky and resizing navigation, a scroll position indicator, smooth scrolling, a carousel with a modal, a fixed background section, a multi-column layout, CSS animations, both horizontal and vertical centering, and a fully playable game embedded live in the Games section below.
